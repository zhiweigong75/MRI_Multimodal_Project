# Skill: project-state

Maintain `docs/project_state.md` as the canonical human/AI handoff document.

Update it when:
- a formal experiment completes
- a major method decision is made
- the active baseline changes
- a known issue is discovered/resolved
- the next experiment plan changes

Keep it concise and current.

Do not turn it into a full lab notebook.
Detailed history belongs in `docs/experiment_log.md` and experiment summaries.
