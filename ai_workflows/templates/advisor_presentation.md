# Advisor Presentation Prompt Template

## Audience
Advisor / PI

## Target duration
TODO minutes

## Primary goal
Support a research decision, not merely summarize activity.

## Source of truth
Use only:
- project state
- experiment registry
- completed experiment summaries
- saved metrics/tables
- relevant methodology
- validated figures

## Narrative
Use a question-driven sequence:

1. What question are we trying to answer?
2. What did we run?
3. What happened?
4. What does the evidence support?
5. What remains uncertain?
6. What decision / next experiment should we discuss?

## Slide behavior
- concise main slides
- detailed methodology in appendix
- trace quantitative claims to saved evidence
- avoid unsupported significance language

- presentation_brief.md

- deck_outline.md
- speaker_notes.md
- editable slides
- build_manifest.json
