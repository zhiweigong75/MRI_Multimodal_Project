# Lab Meeting Presentation Prompt Template

## Audience
Research lab / collaborators

## Target duration
TODO minutes

## Story
Problem → motivation → research question → dataset → method → experiment design → results → interpretation → limitations → next experiments

## Requirements
- Ground quantitative claims in saved experiment evidence.
- Use question-driven result slide titles.
- Explain why each experiment exists.
- Separate evidence from speculation.
- Put detailed implementation/configuration in appendix when possible.
- Include natural speaker notes.
