# Presentation Style

## General

- 16:9 widescreen.
- Clean, editable, academic presentation style.
- White or very light background.
- Generous whitespace.
- Large readable sans-serif typography.
- Restrained navy / teal palette with limited accent colors.
- Minimal decorative elements.
- Avoid crowded slides.

## Narrative

Prefer question-driven titles for research updates.

Examples:
- "Does multimodal fusion improve target-domain prediction?"
- "Did domain alignment improve over the frozen baseline?"
- "Which component is driving the gain?"

## Result slides

A result slide should make the experiment comparison obvious.

Prefer:
- compact tables
- editable plots
- clear baseline highlighting
- one primary message per slide

Do not call small numerical differences significant without statistical support.

## Speaker notes

Notes should explain:
- why the experiment was done
- what changed
- what happened
- what the audience should conclude
- caveats

Do not simply read the slide aloud.

## Appendix

Move:
- exact variable lists
- hyperparameter details
- secondary ablations
- extra fold-level tables
- methodology details

to the appendix when they are not needed for the main story.
