# Research Visual Style

Default visual language for scientific framework, architecture, and pipeline figures.

## Canvas

- 16:9 landscape for presentation-oriented figures unless another ratio is required.
- Clean white canvas.
- Generous whitespace.
- Clear left-to-right or top-to-bottom information flow.

## Typography

- Large readable sans-serif text.
- Strong but restrained hierarchy.
- Avoid tiny labels.

## Palette

- Muted navy / blue for primary computational components.
- Teal for predictions, evidence, or validated outputs when useful.
- Small amber or coral accents for training-only components, warnings, or emphasis.
- Avoid rainbow palettes unless scientifically meaningful.

## Geometry

- Flat vector-like cards/boxes.
- Subtle grouping.
- Thin precise arrows.
- No heavy ornamental borders.
- No 3D.

## Scientific honesty

- Every module and arrow must be supported by repository evidence.
- Training-only signals must not appear as inference inputs.
- Do not invent clinical variables, datasets, labels, metrics, or model components.
- Direction and semantics matter more than decoration.
